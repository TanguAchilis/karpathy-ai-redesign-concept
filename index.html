About

Bio

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator. He was a founding member of OpenAI and later the Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the computer vision team of the Autopilot.

During his PhD at Stanford he was the architect and lead instructor of the first deep learning course at Stanford (CS231n), which has become one of its most popular classes.

For all the latest, I spend most of my time on 𝕏/Twitter or GitHub.
